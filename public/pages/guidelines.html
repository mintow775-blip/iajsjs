Creator Guidelines

Content Requirements

  • All content must be your own original work or properly licensed
  • Provide clear installation instructions
  • Include accurate Minecraft version compatibility
  • Use high-quality project icons and screenshots
  • Write clear, descriptive project descriptions

Prohibited Content

  • Malicious code, viruses, or malware
  • Stolen or copied content without permission
  • Content that violates Minecraft's EULA
  • Inappropriate or offensive material
  • Misleading descriptions or fake downloads

Best Practices

  • Respond to user comments and feedback
  • Keep your projects updated
  • Use semantic versioning for releases
  • Provide changelogs for updates
  • Credit dependencies and inspirations
